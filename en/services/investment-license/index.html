Investment Registration (MISA License)

We prepare, submit and follow up your application until your investment registration certificate is issued.

Home/Services/Investment Registration (MISA License)
← Back to Services

Since the updated Investment Law took effect in February 2025, the official procedure is known as “investment registration”, although many still call it the “MISA license”. It is the mandatory first regulatory step for most foreign investors before starting any formal business activity in Saudi Arabia.

🎯

We Identify the Right Category

Service, commercial, industrial or agricultural — based precisely on your activity.

📋

We Prepare the Full File

We review and organise every required document before submission.

📨

We Submit and Follow Up

We follow the application with the relevant authorities until the certificate is issued.

🔔

We Keep You Informed

Track your application status in real time through our digital platform.
