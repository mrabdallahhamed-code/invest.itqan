Acquiring a Qualified Foreign Company

We handle the acquisition of an existing foreign company that meets the investment registration requirements.

Home/Services/Acquiring a Qualified Foreign Company
← Back to Services

Investment registration applications typically require an existing, qualified foreign company as part of the file. If you do not have one, we identify on your behalf an existing foreign company that meets these requirements, verify its eligibility, and carry out the acquisition in accordance with the applicable regulations.

🔍

We Identify the Right Company

We look for existing foreign companies that fit your activity and the required registration category.

📄

We Verify Eligibility

We confirm the company is established, legally registered and genuinely active, meets the investment registration conditions, and is free of legal issues.

🤝

We Execute the Acquisition

We manage the acquisition procedures in line with applicable regulations and follow every detail on your behalf.

✅

You Receive a Company Ready for the Next Step

With complete, documented paperwork, ready for your investment registration file.
