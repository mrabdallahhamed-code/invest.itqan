Who We Are

Your Specialist Partner for Foreign Investment in Saudi Arabia

Itqan Business Services is a Saudi company founded in Riyadh in 2024. We help foreign investors — individuals and companies — enter the Saudi market with confidence. We handle your entire journey: from investment registration with the Ministry of Investment (the “MISA license”), to establishing your company with full 100% ownership, or even acquiring an existing Saudi company if that is your goal.

Our team combines regulatory expertise in Ministry of Investment (MISA) procedures with close knowledge of the local business environment, shortening your path and reducing the risk of delays or rejection.
