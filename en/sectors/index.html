Sectors Open to Foreign Investment with Full Ownership

Under Vision 2030, the Kingdom has opened most sectors to 100% foreign ownership with no mandatory local partner. Among the most active sectors:

💻

IT and Software

Technology services, software development and digital solutions.

🏗️

Contracting and Construction

Construction projects linked to the Kingdom’s giga-projects.

🛍️

Trade and Retail

Import, distribution and e-commerce of products.

🏭

Industry and Manufacturing

Factories and production facilities with growing government support.

💼

Consulting and Professional Services

Management, financial and engineering consulting.

🏥

Healthcare

Medical facilities and health services.

🎓

Education and Training

Training institutes and education platforms.

🎬

Entertainment and Tourism

A fast-growing sector within Vision 2030.

🌱

Agriculture and Food

Food production and modern agricultural technology.

⚠️ Some sub-activities within each sector may be subject to special requirements or excluded from full ownership. Check with our team to confirm your specific activity.
