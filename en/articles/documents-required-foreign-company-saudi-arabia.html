Documents Required for Investment Registration and Setting Up a Company in Saudi Arabia: A Pre-Submission Checklist

Last updated: June 2026 · Itqan Business Services team

What delays foreign investors’ applications most is documents: missing, unauthenticated or with inconsistent names. Because registration is made in the name of an existing foreign company, the most important documents are that company’s. Here is what you need and how to prepare it from the start.

⚠️ Regulations, conditions and requirements are constantly being renewed and may change after this article’s publication date. What appears here is general information, not legal advice, so we confirm the current requirements for your activity before any submission.

Why Do Applications Stall Over Documents?

The authorities review the application document by document, and any gap or conflict sends it back for completion. Foreign investors also depend on bodies in their own country to issue and authenticate documents, so a single mistake costs weeks, not days. That is why experience in incorporation starts with organising the documents, not with filling in forms.

First: The Foreign Company’s Documents (the Core)

The foreign company is the registered investor, so the file rests on its core documents:

  • The foreign company’s commercial registration in its home country, authenticated.
  • The foreign company’s financial statements for the last fiscal year, authenticated.
  • What proves the company’s details and who represents it.

Why Financial Statements Matter

Financial statements for the last fiscal year are an essential document that cannot be waived, and one of the most often forgotten when preparing the file. A foreign company that has not yet completed a fiscal year, or has no financial statements ready, does not meet this requirement. In that case we acquire an existing, qualified foreign company for you in line with the investment registration conditions rather than delay your project.

Second: Partner and Manager Details

  • Copies of passports for the relevant partners and managers, as applicable.
  • If a partner is a natural person of GCC nationality whose data is not registered in Absher, a copy of their ID is requested.

Third: Activity Documents

Required documents vary with the activity and the type of registration, and activity-specific approvals or conditions may be requested. That is why we define your activity first and then build the document list on it, rather than starting from a generic list.

Fourth: Documents for Incorporating the Company in Saudi Arabia

  • The incorporation contract of the new limited liability company, or its articles of association depending on the entity.
  • Appointment of the manager or managers and their powers.
  • Proof of the company’s premises (a lease or equivalent) and the national address.
  • The partners’ details and shares.

Authentication: Saudi Embassy or Apostille

Documents issued outside the Kingdom need authentication. It is done either by the Saudi embassy in the country of issue, or by Apostille in countries where it is available. The authentication chain differs from one country to another, so know your route from your own country before you start, and do not assume that what works in one country works in another.

What About Translation?

Translation is generally not required for documents submitted to the Ministry of Investment, but other authorities, such as the Ministry of Commerce, may ask in later stages for Arabic documents or a certified translation depending on the procedure.

Checklist Before You Submit

  • Is the foreign company’s commercial registration current and valid?
  • Are the financial statements for the last fiscal year ready?
  • Have the documents been authenticated by the Saudi embassy or by Apostille, according to the country of issue?
  • Do names and dates match across all documents?
  • Is the activity clear, and do you know which type of registration it falls under?
  • Are the partners’ and managers’ details complete?

Important Note

The final list of documents is set by the competent authorities at the time of submission and may change with the activity and the type of registration, so we review your actual requirements with you before any submission.
