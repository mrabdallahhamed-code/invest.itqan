MISA Investment License Renewal: The Annual Update Explained

Last updated: October 2026 · Itqan Business Services team

If you hold an investment license in Saudi Arabia, it has to be kept current. In the Ministry of Investment’s Investor Guide this is done through the annual update of the investment registration, which many people still call “renewing the MISA license”.

Is it a renewal or an annual update?

It is the same thing in practice. The Ministry’s service is titled “Annual Registration Update”, and the guide’s index refers to it as the annual renewal. Whichever name you search for, you complete it through the Ministry’s Invest Saudi e-services.

What the Ministry’s guide says

  • Condition: the foreign partner’s commercial registration in its home country must still be valid.
  • Documents: the guide lists no supporting documents for the standard annual update.
  • Expected service time: 5 working days.
  • Fee: the guide does not publish a fixed amount; the Ministry sets it when the request is accepted and you pay after being notified.
  • Paying on time matters: an update whose fee is not paid within the set period is considered void. The guide gives different payment periods in different sections (15 working days in the service description, 30 days elsewhere), so follow the deadline in your own notification.

A practical checklist before you update

  1. Confirm that the foreign company’s commercial registration in its home country is valid and, if it needs renewing there, renew it first.
  2. Review the data on your registration: activities, capital, partners and the contact officer. Changes are separate services with their own times; the guide lists some as immediate and others as 5 working days.
  3. Put the update date in your calendar well before it falls due.
  4. Pay the fee as soon as you receive the notice.
  5. Keep the confirmation and the updated certificate with your company records.

What happens if you miss the update?

The guide does not list a monetary penalty for a late annual update, but it does state that an unpaid update is considered void. Consequences are set by the Ministry’s regulations and can change, so confirm your position on the e-services portal or contact us before the date passes.

Property-ownership registrations

For non-Saudi companies holding a property-ownership registration, the guide describes a separate annual update that is processed immediately, provided nothing has changed in the company’s country of origin since registration.

Can Itqan handle the update?

Yes. We track your due dates, check the conditions, submit the update, follow the fee notice and keep you informed until the updated registration is confirmed. We do not promise approvals; the decision rests with the Ministry.

⚠️ Requirements, fees and deadlines are set by the Ministry and may change. Check the current Investor Guide or contact us for your case. This page is general information, not legal advice; consult a qualified professional for your situation.

Official sources to verify

Information in this article follows the Ministry’s Investor Guide (13th edition) and should be verified with the official bodies: Ministry of Investment · Investor Guide (PDF) · Invest Saudi e-services
