Service License vs Commercial License for Foreign Investors

Last updated: September 2026 · Itqan Business Services team

One of the most common questions: do I need a service license or a commercial license? The difference is not only the type of activity — it extends to capital requirements and investment commitments.

Service License

Allows providing services only: management and financial consulting, IT, digital marketing, engineering services and HR.

Commercial License

Allows buying, selling, importing and exporting: import and distribution, e-commerce, export, shops and showrooms.

The Key Difference: Requirements

Service: usually lighter capital requirements.

Commercial: usually higher capital and investment requirements, which may differ depending on whether a Saudi partner is involved.

Side-by-Side Comparison

CriterionServiceCommercial
Scope of activityServices onlyBuying, selling, import and export
Import and exportNot permittedPermitted

⚠️ We deliberately give no specific figures here because minimum capital and investment requirements are set by the Ministry of Investment and change over time. Contact us for the current requirements that apply to your activity.
