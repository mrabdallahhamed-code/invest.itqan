Do You Need a Saudi Partner to Set Up a Foreign Company in Saudi Arabia?

Last updated: June 2026 · Itqan Business Services team

One of the questions foreign investors ask most: can I own my company 100%? The short answer: yes in most activities, but there are important exceptions you should know before you build your plan.

⚠️ Regulations, conditions and requirements are constantly being renewed and may change after this article’s publication date. What appears here is general information, not legal advice, so we confirm the current requirements for your activity before any submission.

What Changed with the New Law?

Before the new Investment Law, a foreign investor obtained a prior foreign investment licence. Today the route rests on registration with the Ministry of Investment, while a list of excluded activities and sector regulators’ controls remain. This made full ownership the rule in open activities, but it did not remove the need to study your activity carefully before any step.

The Rule: Full Ownership in Open Activities

The law rests on registration in place of prior licensing, and on treating the foreign investor like the local investor in similar circumstances. In activities open to foreign investment, the registered foreign company can own the Saudi company it incorporates in full, without a Saudi partner.

Exceptions to Watch

  • Excluded activities: there is a list of activities in which a foreign investor may not invest except with special approval from the Ministry.
  • Activities regulated by sector authorities: such as banking, insurance and capital markets; these authorities may set conditions, ownership percentages or further approvals.
  • Some professional and real estate activities: may be subject to special conditions, as with real estate in certain holy cities.

It is enough for your activity to fall in a sector regulated by another authority for you to need its approval or to meet its conditions, even if the activity is open to foreign investment. So it is not enough to ask “is foreign investment allowed?”; ask “which authorities regulate my activity and what are their conditions?”.

When Is a Saudi Partner a Good Option?

Even where full ownership is possible, some investors choose a local partner for commercial reasons:

  • Benefiting from their relationships and knowledge of the local market.
  • Easing entry into certain projects or contracts.
  • Sharing capital and risk in a large project.

The Tax Effect of the Ownership Structure

The make-up of the partners affects the company’s tax obligations: the foreign partner’s share is subject to income tax, while a Saudi or GCC partner’s share is subject to zakat. That is why we study the structure with you before incorporation rather than leaving it to the last minute.

If You Choose a Partner: Protect Yourself in the Contract

Do not rely on the incorporation contract alone; agree in writing on profit distribution, management powers, how partners enter and leave, and dispute resolution. A clear agreement at the start is far cheaper than a later dispute.
