Don’t Dissolve Your Foreign Company After Investment Registration

Last updated: September 2026 · Itqan Business Services team

Many investors, after completing investment registration and setting up their company, consider dissolving the foreign company on the grounds that it has served its purpose. This is a common mistake and can be costly.

Why Is It a Mistake?

The authorities may request periodic updates and refreshed documents for the foreign company, especially when renewing the registration or amending its details.

⚠️ If you close your foreign company or let it lapse, you may be unable to provide updated documents when requested.

What Do “Updated Documents” Mean?

  • A company that remains legally registered and active
  • Able to issue a current commercial registration and recent financial statements
  • Free of new legal issues

What If You Cannot Update the Documents?

Your registration renewal may be delayed, or issues may arise that affect the continuity of your company in Saudi Arabia.

💡 The foreign company is not a tool to be used once and discarded; it is an ongoing part of your obligations for as long as you hold the registration.

How to Avoid It

Keep your company active and compliant, and review any decision affecting it with a qualified adviser. When acquiring a foreign company, scrutinise its legal and financial standing; the Itqan team can help you obtain a qualified, established company from the start.

⚠️ Update requirements may change; always consult a qualified adviser.
