Investment License vs Entrepreneur License in Saudi Arabia

Last updated: September 2026 · Itqan Business Services team

Not every foreign investor is in the same position — an entrepreneur with an early-stage idea differs from an established company seeking expansion. The Ministry of Investment provides two tracks: the investment track and the entrepreneur track.

The Investment Track

Designed for an existing foreign company with a clear financial record that applies for investment registration to establish or expand its business in Saudi Arabia.

The Entrepreneur Track

Does not require an existing foreign company; instead, it relies on a letter from an accredited business incubator or a Saudi university endorsing the project idea.

💡 The entrepreneur track removes the “existing foreign company” barrier and replaces it with endorsement of the project idea by an academic body or accredited incubator.
