Company Setup in Saudi Arabia for Egyptians

The Egyptian investor's guide to setting up a company in Saudi Arabia: whether you need investment registration, documents, steps, cost and how we help.

Does an Egyptian need an investment licence?

An Egyptian is treated as a foreign investor because Egypt is not a GCC member, so you usually start with investment registration (the investment licence) with the Ministry of Investment, then complete incorporation with the Ministry of Commerce. Only GCC nationals register directly with the Ministry of Commerce. Exceptions may apply in some cases, so we review your status before choosing the path.

What options do you have?

A fully foreign-owned company

The most common choice for services, consulting and technology, as far as the activity allows.

A company with a Saudi partner

Suits activities that require Saudi participation, or investors who want a local partner.

A branch of an existing Egyptian company

If you already have a company in Egypt and want to extend it into Saudi Arabia.

Documents typically required

  • A valid passport and the details of the investor and partners.
  • If the investor is a company: the commercial register and latest financial statements, notarised and attested.
  • A description of the intended activity and a brief project outline.
  • A power of attorney for whoever represents you if you do not attend.

Documents issued in Egypt generally need a chain of notarisation and attestation before Saudi authorities accept them. We organise that chain so your file is not delayed.

The steps in brief

  1. 01

    Investment licence

    We prepare the application and follow up until issuance.

  2. 02

    Incorporation

    Articles of association and commercial register.

  3. 03

    Registrations

    Insurance, zakat and tax, Qiwa and national address.

  4. 04

    After incorporation

    HR, Saudization and operations.

Important warnings

  • Do not use commercial concealment (tasattur), meaning registering a business in a Saudi's name while you run it. It is a serious violation with heavy penalties, and the lawful route is available.
  • A residence permit sponsored by an employer does not give you the right to run an independent business.
  • Confirm the activity is not restricted for foreigners before filing.
Cost

How is the setup cost determined?

We do not advertise a fixed price before studying your case, because cost varies with the activity, the entity and the number of partners. But we make clear from the start what you pay and to whom.

Government fees

Paid directly to the official bodies, such as the Ministry of Investment, the Ministry of Commerce, the Chamber of Commerce and licensing authorities. These bodies set them and they can change, so we give you current figures in your proposal.

Itqan's fees

Set after studying your case and according to the agreed scope: investment registration, articles of association and commercial register, and regulatory registrations. You receive them in a clear written proposal before you start.

Frequently asked questions

Can I set up while I am in Egypt?Often yes. Investment registration is online and you can authorise a representative. We review your case to define what needs your attendance.
Do I need a Saudi partner?Not always. It depends on the activity. Some activities allow full foreign ownership; others require Saudi participation.
How long does it take?MISA's guide cites about 10 working days for a new investment registration, then the commercial register and licences are added, depending on how complete your documents are.
