License Types

Investment License Categories

These categories fall under investment registration and differ according to the nature of your activity:

💼

Service License

For providing services only, such as consulting, technology and marketing.

🛍️

Commercial License

For buying, selling, importing and exporting; it usually carries higher capital and investment requirements.

🏭

Industrial License

For manufacturing and production facilities.

🌱

Agricultural License

For agricultural activities and food production.

⚠️ The exact requirements for each category, including minimum capital, are set by the Ministry of Investment and are subject to change. Contact us to learn the requirements that apply to your specific activity. Compare service and commercial licenses
