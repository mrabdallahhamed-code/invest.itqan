FAQ

Answers to the Most Common Questions

Can a foreign investor own 100% of a company in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Under Saudi Vision 2030, a foreign investor can establish a Saudi company with full 100% foreign ownership in most sectors.

Is the “MISA license” still required?

The procedure has been called “investment registration” since the updated Investment Law took effect in February 2025, and the investor receives an investment registration certificate. Many people still call it the “MISA license”.

How long does it take to set up a foreign company in Saudi Arabia?

Investment registration typically takes 5 to 15 working days when documents are complete, followed by the time to issue the articles of association and commercial registration. Total time depends on document completeness and the type of activity.

What is the difference between the service and commercial licenses?

The service license covers services only; the commercial license covers buying, selling, importing and exporting and usually carries higher capital and investment requirements. The Ministry of Investment sets the current requirements.

Does the entrepreneur license require a foreign company?

No existing foreign company is required; it is usually replaced by a letter from an accredited business incubator or a Saudi university.

Why does investment registration require a foreign company?

To show that the investor conducts genuine business activity and has an established record outside the Kingdom.

Can I dissolve my foreign company after registration?

This is not advisable without a regulatory review; the authorities may request periodic updates and refreshed documents for the foreign company.

What if I do not own an existing foreign company?

Specialist firms such as Itqan handle the acquisition of an existing, qualified foreign company that meets the investment registration conditions.
